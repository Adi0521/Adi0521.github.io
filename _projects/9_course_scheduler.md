**Stack:** C++, Python, Node.js, HTML/CSS, Figma

Built in a 4-person Agile/Scrum team. I owned the scheduling backend and the RAG integration, while teammates built the HTML/CSS frontend from a Figma design.

- A micro-scheduling algorithm and APIs that generate, suggest, and return every possible hourly breakdown of a weekly class schedule, integrated with a teammate's semester-level macro-scheduler
- A small LLM-based RAG model that suggests offered courses based on a student's interests and available time slots
